Craps Lab

Privacy Policy for Craps Lab

Last updated 26 August 2026

Craps Lab does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. The only network connection the app makes is to Apple or Google to handle the optional purchase.

What the app stores

Craps Lab saves a small amount of information on your device only, so the game remembers what you were doing between sessions:

  • Any betting strategies you build yourself
  • Your sound on/off preference

This is kept in the app's own private storage using the standard mechanism for app settings on your phone. It never leaves your phone, it is not readable by other apps, and it is deleted when you uninstall Craps Lab.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services of any kind.
  • It does not read your contacts, photos, location, files, camera, or microphone.
  • It does not process payments itself. Apple or Google handles the purchase.
  • It does not share, sell, or disclose information to anyone, because it holds none.

Permissions

Craps Lab requests no Android runtime permissions — you will never see a permission prompt.

The app's manifest lists INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and Google Play's billing permission. These are used for one thing only: the optional in-app purchase, and checking whether your account already owns it. The dice, the payouts and the strategy simulation all run entirely on your device and do not need a network connection.

Purchases

Craps Lab offers one optional in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google, depending on where you installed the app. We never receive or store your name, email address, postal address or payment details. The app records only whether the unlock has been purchased, and that record is kept on your device. Restoring a purchase asks the store whether your account owns it. That request goes to Apple or Google, not to us.

Children

Craps Lab simulates a casino dice game. There is no gambling and no real-money wagering: no deposits, no cash prizes, and nothing of value can be won or lost. The only payment is the optional one-time unlock described above. It is not directed at children, and because it collects nothing, no information from any user of any age is gathered.

Real money

Craps Lab is a simulation and a strategy tester. It uses play money only. It is not a gambling service, does not accept wagers, and does not pay out winnings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Craps Lab adds anything that handles your information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@emptysea.app, also listed on the Craps Lab page on Google Play and the App Store.

Craps Lab is published by EmptySea. This policy covers the Craps Lab application on Android and iOS (com.emptysea.craps) and no other product.
