What we make

Casino education apps, for Android and iOS.

Most casino apps do one of two things. They simulate a table so you can pass the time, or they sell you a betting system and tell you it works. Ours do a third thing: they measure. Pick any betting system and the app runs it thousands of times against the exact table rules you chose, then shows you the house edge, how often you finish ahead, how often you go broke, and how much money you actually need to survive a session.

The numbers are checked against published casino mathematics, and the same engine that runs the simulation runs the game you play — so what the app measures and what it deals can't drift apart. Where the honest number is unflattering, the app leads with it.
